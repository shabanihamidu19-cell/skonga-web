What is the AI Chat feature in SKONGA AI?
Ask questions in plain language and get explanations aimed at secondary school level.
  • Works for homework, revision, and “I don’t understand this topic”
  • Better results when you name the subject and what is confusing
  • Soft free limits; Pro unlocks more usage
Does SKONGA AI support scan and solve for exam questions?
Capture a written or printed question and use AI-powered help to work through it.
  • Useful when typing a long problem is slow
  • Allow camera permission when the app asks
  • Always learn the steps — don’t only copy the final answer
How does Form and Combination work in SKONGA AI?
Set your form (1–6). For Form 5–6, optionally choose your subject combination (tahasusi).
  • Keeps suggestions aligned with your level
  • A-level combinations (e.g. PCM, HGE) avoid off-subject topics
  • You can change this later in profile where available
How do SKONGA AI Pro and mobile money payments work?
Optional Pro plans via the in-app payment sheet (M-Pesa, Tigo, Airtel, Halo, etc.).

  • After pay, look for green “Active until…”
  • If unlock is slow, stay online and use sync / “I paid”
