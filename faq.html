Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about SKONGA AI — the student AI assistant built in Tanzania.

What is SKONGA AI?
SKONGA AI is an independent AI-powered student assistant for Tanzanian secondary learners (Form 1–6). It helps with questions, explanations, scan-style help, and form plus subject-combination awareness. It is a consumer study app, not school administration software.
Is SKONGA AI the same as the Skonga school ERP?
No. SKONGA AI is a student-facing AI study assistant. It is not related to institutional “Skonga” school management, ERP, admissions, fees, or LMS products used by schools and colleges. See our About page.
Who can use SKONGA AI?
Secondary students in Tanzania, especially Form 1–6. Form 5–6 users can set their subject combination (tahasusi) so help stays relevant to their subjects.
How do I install the Android APK?
Download the latest signed release APK from the download section or GitHub Releases, open it on Android, and allow install from that source if asked. Play Protect may warn on first install of apps outside Play Store — use the official SKONGA release only.
How do I update an older APK?
Download the newest APK and install it over the previous version. When online, the app can also check for updates and prompt you.
Is SKONGA AI free?
There is a free tier with soft limits. Optional Pro unlocks more usage via mobile money (e.g. M-Pesa, Tigo, Airtel, Halo) in the app.
What is Form and Combination?
You can choose your form (1–6). For Form 5–6 you may optionally select your subject combination (tahasusi), for example HGE or PCM, so suggestions match your subjects.
Who builds SKONGA AI?
Founded by Shabani Hamidu, published under KCL Platform TZ — an independent EdTech project built in Tanzania.
