About

What is SKONGA AI?

SKONGA AI is an independent AI-powered student assistant built for secondary school learners in Tanzania (Form 1–6). It helps students ask questions, understand topics, scan academic problems, and study with awareness of their form level and subject combination (tahasusi).

SKONGA AI is a consumer learning product — installed as an Android APK or used in the browser — not software for school administrators.

Not a school management system

SKONGA AI is not related to products or websites that use the name “Skonga” for school management, ERP, admissions, fees, or institutional LMS (for example systems aimed at schools and colleges). Those platforms serve schools as organizations. SKONGA AI serves students directly with AI study help.

What SKONGA AI does

  • AI chat for explanations matched to secondary-school level
  • Optional scan-and-solve style help for written questions
  • Form selection and Form 5–6 subject combination (tahasusi) personalization
  • Soft free usage with optional Pro via mobile money (e.g. M-Pesa, Tigo, Airtel, Halo)
  • Android APK distribution and in-app update checks
